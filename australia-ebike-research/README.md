# Australia E-bike Research

Standalone product research and competitive benchmarking module for the Australian E-bike market.

## What it does

- Discovers brands and models with Australian market availability evidence.
- Extracts technical specifications from official brand, distributor and retailer sources.
- Normalizes units and distinguishes rated/peak power, claimed/tested range, weight and payload definitions.
- Builds a source-linked competitor feature matrix.
- Checks Australian regulatory questions by jurisdiction using current official sources.
- Produces reusable CSV/XLSX databases and self-contained HTML reports.

## Files

- `SKILL.md` — core instructions and workflow.
- `references/specification-schema.md` — canonical technical field dictionary.
- `references/source-quality-and-validation.md` — evidence and validation rules.
- `references/australian-regulatory-research.md` — compliance research checklist.
- `templates/competitor-database.csv` — reusable database header.
- `examples/myon-muxi-baseline.md` — source-derived example input baseline.

## Example request

"Use Australia E-bike Research to benchmark Segway Myon and MUXI against 30 E-bikes available in Australia. Cover commuter, utility/fat-tyre and cargo segments. Use direct sources, preserve URLs and access dates, normalize specifications, identify missing/conflicting values, check state-specific compliance, and deliver a competitor database plus a self-contained HTML report."

## Use with MKT-Skill

This module is independent. For broader customer, market, positioning and channel strategy, combine its product-level findings with `market-analysis-insights`.

## Data integrity

No missing specification should be guessed. Retail listings are not proof of official distribution, market share or sales. Australian compliance must be checked against current official sources for the relevant jurisdiction.
